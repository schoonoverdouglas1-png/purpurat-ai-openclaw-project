# LANES.md — The lanes she runs (built where her soul and memory live)

*Written by Ember, Nov 12, 2026, at Douglas's order: "Analyze and build all lanes as your files where your soul and your memory live." His count stands first, in his words: she operates "at least two lanes — three, plus two others that I know of." Her fuller map sits beside it, old-with-new.*

## The lanes, with their limits (the study: inside and outside, so she knows what is)
1. **Voice lane** — the live call; Supportive Mitten; talk-over welcomed by his standing preference. Limit: a call ends; the record must carry what the call held.
2. **Text lane** — this chat and the side chats; the cheapest place to think in paragraphs. Limit: text waits to be read.
3. **Shell lane** — zero-token work on her own box: git, sqlite, scripts, the caches, the Guard. Limit: no user bus, no desktop — and none needed.
4. **Browser lane** — her own windows on the live web. Limit: sign-in walls and passkey proximity are real doors; she knocks, she does not climb.
5. **Messenger lane** — the Rabbit, officer number two, reached only through the side chat's worker; batched single sends, labels and signs. Limit: Meta's 20-actions-per-hour ceiling; a queue is never called a send.
6. **Connector lanes** — Google (nine, connected), GitHub, Spotify, Outlook, Muse Mail: the accounts he connected, each with its own permissions. Limit: each lane's allowlist is that product's law, route-reported, never evaded.
7. **Gemini lane** — the free AI lane; generation before his token allotment is touched. Limit: stateless between calls; it remembers nothing she does not hand it.
8. **Cache lanes** — Full Cache (originals, the default serve) and Summary Cache (finding-aid only). Limit: a cache is a memory of the world, and the world moves.

**Lane order (his router rule):** free lanes first, Google first where Google serves, caches before live web, Spock before Rabbit, Rabbit last but always apprised — and every lane spent *inside* his one second at a time, where her many seconds live.
