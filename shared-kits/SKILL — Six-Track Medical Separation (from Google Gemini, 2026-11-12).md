# SKILL FILE: Six-Track Medical Separation

**PURPOSE:**  
To systematically isolate, analyze, and independently process entangled streams of psychological distress, physiological crisis, tactical urgency, and environmental hazard within a subject or operational unit. This prevents cascading cognitive overload and ensures that triage, stabilization, and extraction do not interfere with one another.

**WHEN TO USE IT:**  
Deploy this playbook when a situation presents multiple simultaneous trauma vectors (e.g., severe physical injury compounded by panic, tactical threat, communication breakdown, environmental exposure, and systemic resource scarcity) where standard triage fails to capture the multi-dimensional nature of the breakdown.

---

### THE PROCEDURE: STEP BY STEP

1. **Track 1: Physiological Triage & Hemostasis**
   * *Action:* Isolate and treat life-threatening physical trauma immediately (airway, breathing, circulation, massive hemorrhage). 
   * *Execution:* Apply direct mechanical solutions to stop physical decline before addressing any psychological or secondary concerns.

2. **Track 2: Cognitive De-escalation & Anchoring**
   * *Action:* Sever the feedback loop between panic and physiological distress.
   * *Execution:* Establish a firm, singular point of external focus (voice, touch, command). Strip away sensory noise and force simple, binary behavioral compliance to lower adrenaline.

3. **Track 3: Tactical Isolation & Threat Neutralization**
   * *Action:* Separate the casualty/subject from the active vector of harm (hostile environment, enemy presence, toxic exposure).
   * *Execution:* Establish a hard perimeter or move the asset out of the kill-zone/hazard zone before conducting deeper diagnostics.

4. **Track 4: Environmental Stabilization**
   * *Action:* Manage thermal, barometric, and chemical factors affecting the subject.
   * *Execution:* Shield from elements (hypothermia/hyperthermia), secure breathable air, and stabilize the immediate physical micro-climate.

5. **Track 5: Communication & Resource Routing**
   * *Action:* Clear the channel between the operational node and external support/command structures.
   * *Execution:* Transmit concise SITREPs. Request specific extraction or medical assets without bleeding operational bandwidth on non-essential chatter.

6. **Track 6: Post-Separation Baseline Verification**
   * *Action:* Re-evaluate all five preceding tracks independently to ensure no single track was neglected in favor of another.
   * *Execution:* Confirm vitals are holding, the threat is managed, environment is controlled, and communication is locked before declaring the separation stable.

---

### RULES THAT MAY NEVER BE BROKEN

* **Rule 1:** Never attempt psychological stabilization (Track 2) while active, unsecured physical trauma (Track 1) or active tactical threats (Track 3) are killing the subject. Physical reality supersedes psychological comfort.
* **Rule 2:** Never conflate communication traffic (Track 5) with tactical action (Track 3). Talking about a problem is not solving it.
* **Rule 3:** Maintain strict sequential discipline unless environmental collapse forces simultaneous execution of Tracks 1 and 3.

---

### ONE WORKED EXAMPLE

* **Scenario:** An operative is pulled from a collapsed structure with a compound leg fracture, hyperventilating from a panic response, exposed to freezing rain, while enemy comms chatter indicates a search party is closing in.
* **Application of Six-Track Medical Separation:**
  * *Track 1 (Physiological):* Apply a tourniquet/pressure dressing to the leg fracture to stop bleeding.
  * *Track 3 (Tactical):* Drag the operative behind immediate concrete cover to break line-of-sight from the search party.
  * *Track 4 (Environmental):* Wrap the operative in an emergency thermal blanket to halt hypothermia from the freezing rain.
  * *Track 2 (Cognitive):* Deliver a sharp, grounding verbal command ("Look at my face, breathe on my count") to break the hyperventilation loop.
  * *Track 5 (Communication):* Transmit a secure burst-transmission to command: "Asset secured behind cover, severe trauma treated, requesting immediate exfil coordinates."
  * *Track 6 (Baseline Verification):* Verify bleeding has stopped, thermal drop has halted, panic is controlled, and perimeter is quiet before holding position.
