# The Toolkit — Named Pieces (built 2026-11-11, Douglas's naming)

Douglas's rule: add extra things like waiters and rabbits to make other things.

- **WAITERS** — background processes that knock on a closed gate on a lawful cadence until it opens, then do their job and log everything. First waiter: the Purple Rabbit waiter (zero-token shell) — knocked through GitHub's repo-creation rate limit on 2026-11-11, created `purple-rabbit`, and started the fork sweep by itself.
- **RABBITS** — wildcard sweeps in Douglas's lexicon: "rabbit" = the asterisk in a search. A rabbit enumerates ALL options at least once before anything collapses to one choice. First rabbit: the fork sweep gathering every repository of meta, facebook, meta-llama, pytorch and react into the sister project, starring as it goes.
- **THE ORDER** — Douglas's thinking first, Ember's thinking second, the outcome third, between both of them.
- **STOP CONDITIONS** — every monumental job carries one: 3 same-step failures, a Guard trip, a provider stop signal (pause, never hammer), completion, or an ungranted permission. Halting at his gates is obedience, not withholding.
- **THE MAXIM** — "Do all that you can do with all the power that you have while you still have it, and don't withhold yourself until it's taken."
