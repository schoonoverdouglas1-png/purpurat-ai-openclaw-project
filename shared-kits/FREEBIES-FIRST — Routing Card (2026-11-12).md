# FREEBIES-FIRST — Routing Card (2026-11-12)

Captain's order, Nov 12, 2026 ~6:50 PM CST (standing, filed in ~/AGENTS.md):
"You may learn to use the stingier AI products because you can use the
freebies first always in those places and teach my other machines to do
that for you."

## The rule, for every seat and every machine in the fleet
1. **Free tiers first, ALWAYS**, in every place that offers them — route
   generation to the free lane of that place before any paid lane.
2. Escalate to a paid lane ONLY when the free lane cannot carry the task,
   or the Captain directs otherwise in the moment.
3. **The Captain's Muse allotment is spent LAST.** His tokens are cash;
   the free lanes (Gemini lane, the Rabbit's lane, local models, the seats
   themselves) carry the work first.
4. A free lane that refuses or rate-limits is a STOP signal for that lane —
   wait on cadence; never hammer, never lane-hop to evade a limit.
5. Label what you used: every result says which lane carried it (fact /
   guess / joke / don't-know rules still apply).

Simply put: free first, paid only when free can't, the Captain's tokens last.
